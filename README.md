# 📊 Executive Summary & Profitability Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Executive Summary & Profitability Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, revenue generation, and profitability across different product categories, countries, regions, and sales channels.

It provides a consolidated view of key business performance indicators (KPIs), helping stakeholders monitor financial performance, identify revenue trends, and make data-driven business decisions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Monitor overall sales and financial performance.
- Analyze monthly revenue and gross profit trends.
- Evaluate profitability and cost of goods sold (COGS).
- Compare revenue across different sales channels.
- Understand regional and geographical sales performance.
- Provide an interactive interface for business analysis.

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard tracks six major business metrics:

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| Total Revenue | Overall revenue generated from sales |
| Total Gross Profit | Revenue remaining after deducting COGS |
| Gross Profit Margin | Percentage of revenue retained as gross profit |
| Total Units Sold | Total number of units sold |
| Total Orders | Total number of customer orders |
| COGS | Total Cost of Goods Sold |

## 📊 Dashboard Visualizations

The dashboard includes the following visual components:

**1. Monthly Revenue and Profit**
- Tracks monthly revenue and gross profit.
- Helps identify seasonal trends and monthly performance changes.

**2. Revenue by Sales Channel**
- Compares revenue across Corporate, Marketplace, Online Store, and Retail Partners.
- Helps evaluate sales channel performance.

**3. Sales Channel Distribution**
- Pie chart showing the contribution of different sales channels.

**4. Region-Wise Comparison**
- Displays regional revenue distribution.
- Helps understand geographical business performance.

**5. Country-Wise Map Chart**
- Provides a geographical view of revenue distribution across countries.

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters (Slicers)

The dashboard includes interactive filters to support dynamic analysis:

- **Product Category:** Camping, Cycling, Fitness, Hiking, Travel.
- **Country:** Filter performance by individual countries.
- **Quarter:** Q1, Q2, Q3, and Q4.

These filters allow users to explore business performance based on specific categories, geographical locations, and reporting periods.

## 💡 Business Insights

The dashboard helps answer important business questions:

- How is revenue changing month over month?
- Which sales channels contribute the most revenue?
- What is the overall gross profit margin?
- Which regions and countries generate revenue?
- How do product categories perform across different markets?
- What is the relationship between revenue and the cost of goods sold?

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Data Analysis: Pivot Tables
- Data Visualization: Charts, Slicers, Conditional Formatting, Map charts
- Data Processing: Flash fill, Data Validation, Formulas and Functions

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Open the dashboard using the appropriate application.
2. Review the KPI cards to understand overall performance.
3. Explore monthly revenue and profit trends.
4. Use the interactive slicers to filter the data.
5. Compare sales channel and regional performance.
6. Analyze geographical revenue distribution.
